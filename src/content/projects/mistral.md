I build the **internal corporate platform** at Mistral, on the **Enterprise Agents** team.

The platform uses early Mistral products (models, agents, automation primitives) before they ship externally, and turns them into concrete tooling for the teams that run the company. The teams I serve directly are **Finance**, **Customer Support**, **HR**, **Solutions**, and **Go-to-Market**.

## What I do

- Ship automations and internal projects across those five teams, currently **20+ in flight**, so hard to list them all individually
- Act as an early user of Mistral's own products: I break them, give feedback, and help shape what eventually reaches customers
- Cover the full loop end-to-end: identifying the operational pain, building the automation, and getting it into the hands of the team

## Tech

- **Backend**: Python and Node.js, depending on the automation
- **Platform**: Mistral's own models, agents, and early internal tooling

## Outcome

Real internal teams use this every day to close tickets, process financial flows, and unblock their own work with AI instead of manual handling.
