I led the engineering team behind the backoffice application for **L by LCL**, LCL's digital banking offering.

The users were customer-service agents. The app let them operate on customer accounts and automated a set of legal and regulatory operations that were previously manual, things like *saisie sur comptes* (account garnishment) and handling *huissiers* (bailiff) claims, plus the legal reportings tied to them.

## What I did

- Wrote the first line of the codebase and grew it from there
- Led a team of **4 developers** at its peak
- Owned the scope end-to-end with the customer-service teams as my clients
- Stayed on the project for **1 year and 3 months**

## Tech

- **Frontend**: React
- **Backend**: Fastify on Node.js, with Kafka for event streaming and SSE to push live updates to the agents' screens
- **Infra**: Kubernetes (as a consumer of the platform team's cluster, not running DevOps myself)

## Outcome

When I left, the application had **15 daily users** operating on **more than 20,000 accounts**.
